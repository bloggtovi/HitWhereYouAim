## Ballistic Trajectory Solver

The ballistic trajectory solver is based on the public-domain
implementation by Forrest Smith and was rewritten into generic
C++ for Hit Where You Aim.

The polynomial root-solving functions are based on code by
Jochen Schwarze from Graphics Gems I and are used under the
Graphics Gems License.
